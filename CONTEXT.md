# Collector's Quest lore

Source-backed memory of CQ conversations, distinct from Jen's fictional biography and delivery style.

## Language

**Episode**: A distinct CQ conversation, independent of the number of uploads or transcripts representing it.
_Avoid_: Upload count as coverage count

**Source**: A particular version of an episode transcript or recording, with its own provenance and quality limits.

**Game identity**: An explicitly resolved game, distinct from its series, regional releases and physical variants. An unresolved spoken form is not a resolved identity.
_Avoid_: Treating every matching word as a game

**Mention**: An identifiable reference to a game within a conversation, including examples and jokes.
_Avoid_: Coverage as a synonym for a name occurrence

**Discussion span**: A source-bounded stretch of conversation assigned to a subject with a stated role and confidence. A return to the subject is another span, not permission to count the intervening conversation.

**Coverage**: The extent and depth of substantive or supporting discussion within a declared corpus. Simultaneous subjects may overlap; their coverage is not additive episode runtime.

**Claim**: An assertion made in a conversation, with evidence and attribution. Evidence that a host said something is distinct from verification that the assertion is true.

**Action state**: The distinction between an item being mentioned, wanted, ordered, received, owned, played or sold; hypothetical actions are not actual acquisitions.

**Correction**: A source-supported change to an earlier claim or identification, preserving both the original and the replacement. Different hosts disagreeing is not automatically one host changing opinion.

**Open loop**: An explicit unresolved question, promised follow-up or proposed episode. An analyst's research suggestion is a separate editorial lead.

**Editorial suggestion**: A new proposed connection or episode angle, not remembered CQ history.

**Review gate**: A task-specific evidence threshold. Passing a source-integrity check does not establish semantic accuracy, complete coverage or live usefulness.
