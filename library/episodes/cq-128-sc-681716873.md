## Publisher description

On this show we've got 30 game collecting trivia questions we're asking each other from variants to launch titles to box art. And we're trying to keep it a little real here, not well known facts like "What is the Famicom game that SMB2 is based on?". Play along at home and let us know how much smarter you are on the spot than we are at the very thing we talk about week in and out! While it was fun to quiz each other, it's also a good way to bring up and discuss topics that won't ever get their own show, like R-Zone hardware revisions!
