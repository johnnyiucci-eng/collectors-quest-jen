## Publisher description

In this episode we're mostly continuing our last discussion and talking about the future of gaming collecting, changing attitudes towards what is important to collect, set collecting, speculation, the physical space needed to for collections full of bulk, modern games, art... The episode started out as the rising importance of pure demand vs. rarity in game prices, but we kind of go everywhere. If you like listening to us talk about this kind of thing, here's a whole lot more of it!
