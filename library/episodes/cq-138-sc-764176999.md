## Publisher description

When someone dies (or perhaps just when they're in the news) memorabilia related to them goes up in price. In this episode we're talking about the multiple things that cause this and how significant it is to video games. Of particular note is Kobe Bryant who recently unexpectedly passed away causing unprecedented spikes on otherwise undesirable games.
