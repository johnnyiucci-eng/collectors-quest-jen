# Pilot pre-show brief: completing a collection without letting it take over

Draft editorial aid, September 12, 2026. Uses the ten pilot episodes only. Episode statements are not independently verified facts. Timestamps identify caption windows, not exact utterances.

## Useful opening

“A missing slot used to mean buying another game. When does filling it stop making the collection better?”

This connects the early set-completion discussion to later changes in taste, completeness and buying restraint. It gives both hosts room to disagree without assuming they share one collecting philosophy.

## Fresh angles

1. Ask for one real example where a placeholder worked and one where it doubled the eventual expense. Separate a personal result from a price prediction.
2. Compare buying restraint with cataloging: did better documentation resolve a missing slot, or reveal five new ones?
3. Ask which completion rules each host would now discard. Preserve individual answers instead of inventing a consensus.

## A strong closing anecdote

CQ 300, 03:48:16–03:52:21: Johnny bought another Terror compilation because he thought Jack the Ripper was missing. It was identified on the back of an existing floppy. He retracted his mistaken message to the seller. This illustrates inspection and documentation, not seller dishonesty. [Transcript](../library/episodes/cq-300-sc-2396296749.md#034816).

## Guardrails for delivery

- Host eras matter: CQ 6/7 are Kat and Johnny; CQ 146/200 include Stefan. Do not default every older anecdote to Tyler.
- Distinguish owned, purchased-but-not-arrived, wanted and hypothetical items. Witchaven's prize-copy origin in CQ 300 remains inconclusive.
- Current prices, counterfeit determinations and historical firsts need their own verification before practical advice.
- Tyler's CQ 300 break has no agreed return date. Christmas/2027 is Johnny's proposed time to revisit discussion; January 2028 is a joke.
- The brief has not been tested in live delivery or rated for usefulness. It does not activate or alter Jen's persona.
