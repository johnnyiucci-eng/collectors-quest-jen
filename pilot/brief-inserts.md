# Completing the right copy

Draft preparation brief from CQ 248, published May 24, 2024. Source-linked claims describe the recorded discussion; prices and precise product codes have not been independently refreshed or verified. The source's timestamps identify caption windows, not exact utterance starts.

The useful starting point is that a game can have a cartridge, manual and box while still missing its correct components. Tecmo's quick-reference sheets are game- and platform-specific; similar names do not make Baseball and Super Baseball interchangeable. [Tecmo discussion](../library/episodes/cq-248-sc-1829470383.md#002536).

Product codes help, but a matching revision number across the box and manual is not a rule: the parts could be revised independently, and some start at -1. This is a good place for Jen to explain a concrete exception without treating every variation as an error. [Revision qualification](../library/episodes/cq-248-sc-1829470383.md#002028).

Mega Man 3 supplies a clear object example. The hosts distinguish its Mega Man refund slip from generic Capcom/Captain Commando refunds and discuss a later box with the offer removed. Collecting the later version is also presented as a valid choice. [Refund discussion](../library/episodes/cq-248-sc-1829470383.md#010621).

The underlying collector question is whether this game deserves more attention in the collection. Tyler's challenge is that selling an unwanted game can be as valid as chasing its missing insert; Johnny agrees that making it nicer assumes the collector has decided to keep it. [Ownership decision](../library/episodes/cq-248-sc-1829470383.md#002840).

Avoid three traps on mic: the maple-leaf Zelda seal is a joke; Bill Elliott's promotional box being first print is a stated heuristic; Amazon distribution of the Baten Kaitos soundtrack is expressly speculation. Keep those uncertainties attached to the examples. [Zelda bit](../library/episodes/cq-248-sc-1829470383.md#003849), [first-print qualification](../library/episodes/cq-248-sc-1829470383.md#005506), [Amazon theory](../library/episodes/cq-248-sc-1829470383.md#012337).

A fresh angle to explore is how a collector decides which missing components are worth pursuing. This is a proposed angle, not a finding that CQ has never covered it. Broad coverage and saturation judgments await the remaining pilot episodes.
