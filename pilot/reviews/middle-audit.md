The central lesson is not “one perfect website”: use sources for the evidence they actually contain. Tyler values Usenet for historical opinions, not authoritative truth; Johnny values Game-Rave and SNES Central for specialized research. [00:20:29–00:23:33; 00:52:10–00:56:17](../../library/episodes/cq-146-sc-852741172.md).
