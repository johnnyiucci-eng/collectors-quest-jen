Odyssey 2 looks cheap in total while Tyler still struggles to source a few games; PS2 and Xbox look cheap per game but have large libraries. All figures are dated 2017, not current advice. [P7–10,20–21,28–31,38](../../../library/episodes/cq-075-sc-343883458.md).
