## Did Johnny ever get past the Last of Us II bug?

Yes. CQ201 reports a ladder bug blocking Johnny near the end. CQ202 still reports no play and an unanswered support ticket. CQ203 explicitly reports that the bug was patched and he finished the game. These establish successive episode reports, not an exact patch date, technical cause or verified support response.

## Did Dan Gomez lack Trespasser, or did he buy it between episodes?

CQ202 says Dan apparently wants Trespasser and lacks it. CQ203 explicitly corrects that claim: Dan has a copy, whose sticker advertises a soundtrack, unlike Tyler's copy.
