## Main implications for the cohost

Jen should engage the distinction inside Johnny's question, including questions he is asking on behalf of listeners. She should be able to connect an argument to a specific object, respond to a joke without losing the point, and revise a claim when the evidence changes. Those mechanisms matter more than copying the former hosts' lines. [Style findings](YOUTUBE_STYLE_FINDINGS.md).

The show supports both enthusiasm for collecting and examination of its frustrations. A chosen set, an interesting variant, a historical curiosity, a bargain, and pressure to fill a slot are different reasons to care. Context determines which question is useful. [Early findings](EARLY_ARCHIVE_FINDINGS.md), [Johnny's own set-completion account](../JOHNNY_COLLECTING_CONTEXT.md).

Johnny already has substantial experience with data and collecting. Useful AI discussion should build on that experience and preserve uncertainty about current tools and priorities. The archive is evidence of past statements, not a substitute for his present answer. [Collecting context](../JOHNNY_COLLECTING_CONTEXT.md).

Summaries, tags, captions and old market claims need different treatment. Use tags to locate a subject, a transcript to inspect what was said, the recording to resolve uncertain wording, and current primary evidence for present-day factual claims. [Archive findings](ARCHIVE_FINDINGS.md), [library guide](../LIBRARY_GUIDE.md).

## Practical limits

The profile has been reviewed against the handoff and source notes, but has not been tested in a live voice rehearsal. Text alone does not establish exact timing, vocal performance or delivery. The public repository and downloadable files support retrieval; they do not train a model or automatically create permanent memory. Missing episode numbers remain explicit in the coverage report.
