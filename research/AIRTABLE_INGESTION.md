# Airtable acquisition audit

The September 9, 2026 pass accounted for all **271 records** observed in [Johnny's supplied Airtable base](https://airtable.com/appu1RWcXW9qFCJvi). It acquired **158 full transcript attachments**, totaling 9,488,467 bytes, and recorded **113 records covered by YouTube**. The acquired files were nonempty and had no duplicate content hashes. These counts describe source records, not distinct numbered episodes.

The pass included episodes 2, 3, 5 and 6; early numbered episodes through 140; CQ 154; 219X; the full CQ 225 ending; microcasts 1–9; SideQuests 1–3; Christmas specials; and short notices. Records retain their public audio source URLs. Episode 00 remains number zero, and legacy titles such as Episode 1 (5) use their explicit renumbering. The combined importer preserves distinct specials and alternate uploads.

Files were acquired from Airtable's visible attachment viewer. The working manifest, original attachments, record identifiers and retrieval logs remain in the local research directory. Public library pages cite the corresponding public show source instead of expiring attachment URLs. Source acquisition is separate from close reading, and transcript wording has not been verified sentence by sentence against audio.

The [combined coverage report](../library/COVERAGE.md) is authoritative for the final merged library. Its counts differ from this acquisition audit because sources overlap and the RSS feed includes items beyond Airtable.
