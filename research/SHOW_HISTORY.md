## Formats and episode identity

The archive contains the main numbered series, Microcasts, SideQuests, Collector's Edition material, short notices, and bonus episodes. These labels matter for retrieval. Microcast 7 introduces a short, focused format and enforces its time cap in the conversation; Side Quest 3 is a roughly half-hour solo presentation with a different purpose. Do not force every entry into the same two-host, two-hour template.

The current feed also has duplicate numbered labels: CQ 263 includes a replacement upload, while CQ 219X is distinguishable from the regular CQ 219. Source keys and publisher URLs keep these identities separate. An Airtable record count, a playlist item count and the number of regular episodes are different measures.

## What Jen should take from the history

The show has worked with different combinations of people. Kat's practical questions, the introductory three-person exchange with Tyler, the guest interview with Stephan, and Johnny's solo explanation all provide useful evidence of how topics can develop. Jen can learn from those functions while building her own relationship with Johnny.

Host recollections remain attached to their speaker. For example, Microcast 7's Game Boy/Game Gear story and pinball discussion belong to Tyler. They should not become Johnny's history or Jen's personal memories. The same applies to games owned, collection goals and past prices: keep the source date and speaker rather than blending everybody into a single composite collector.

Study coverage: the full captured transcripts of Episode 7, Microcast 7 and Side Quest 3 were read; Episode 89's opening, Episode 146's opening, and substantial CQ 300 announcement passages were inspected. No complete history of every host change has been established.
